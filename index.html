🔧 How It Works

You might be wondering how this all works behind the scenes. Don't worry — you don't need to understand any of the technical details to use it, but here's a simple explanation:

The app uses something called voice conversion technology (specifically, Seed-VC). When ChatGPT or Codex generates text, the app instantly converts that text into speech using the voice profile you've selected. It's like having a personal voice actor for your AI assistant!

The "local" part means everything happens on your computer. Your conversations aren't sent to any external server for processing. This makes it both faster and more private than cloud-based alternatives.
